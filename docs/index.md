      <section class="tui-panel tui-faq" id="faq" data-tui-panel aria-labelledby="tui-faq-title"><h2 id="tui-faq-title">Questions</h2><details><summary>Do I need Google?</summary><p>No. Local accounts, notes, local files and projects work without Google. Classroom import and Google-backed document workflows need their respective integration.</p></details><details><summary>Where are my files stored?</summary><p>In the instance’s local storage or the administered Drive, depending on the workflow. A local backup does not download files that exist only on Drive.</p></details><details><summary>Does it send my work to an AI service?</summary><p>No, not by itself. The AI assistant is off until an administrator connects a model server the instance already uses, such as Ollama on the local network. People then choose which notes or files to include.</p></details><details><summary>Is hosting free?</summary><p>The source is open under AGPL-3.0-only. You provide the server, domain and any external services; those can have their own costs.</p></details><details><summary>Can I use ZimaOS, CasaOS or UmbrelOS?</summary><p>Compose instructions are provided for compatible hosts. These are manual deployments, not official one-click app-store packages.</p></details><details><summary>Can I use a phone?</summary><p>The workspace and documentation adapt to smaller screens. Some tasks, such as editing a large board or administering a server, are easier with more space.</p></details><footer class="tui-about"><p>Independent, open-source study software.</p><a href="LICENSING/">License & credits</a></footer></section>
