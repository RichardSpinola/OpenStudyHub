# OpenStudyHub 3.2.2

Fixes for generated documents and a refreshed look for the Extras. Use the pinned `ghcr.io/richardspinola/openstudyhub:3.2.2` image.

## Documents

- Renaming a generated document from OpenStudyHub now renames the Drive file through the account that owns it, so shared copies no longer go back to the old name.
- Unchecked optional sections, such as Summary or Questions, are removed from the generated Google Doc, also for starters.
- The class-notes starter names files with the typed title, and starters that were already imported appear only once in the template picker.

## App

- The welcome wizard has four steps instead of six; notifications and appearance are adjusted later in Settings.
- The product tour includes the AI assistant when AI is enabled.
- The chat and the AI assistant no longer link to each other twice. Old `/chat?assistant=1` links open the assistant.
- At medium screen widths the top navigation shows icons only, so links no longer slide under the clock and utilities.
- The Home customize links open the Home and shortcuts settings.
- Chat message menus open downward when the top of the list would cut them off.

## Extras

- Memory, Sudoku, Connect Four and Tic-tac-toe have covers and boards drawn like the real table games, and each study tool has its own tint.
- Minesweeper is easier on phones: the board scrolls inside its frame, rows keep the same height as cells open, and flag mode is a large toggle.

## Updating

No database changes. Update from 3.2.0 or 3.2.1 by switching the image; from older versions, follow the [update guide](../docs/backup-update.md).
